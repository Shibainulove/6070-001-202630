# MSSP 6070 · Assignment 1: Academic Performance of Engineering Students

Case study for the Department of Education. It looks at how parental education, socioeconomic stratum, gender and other attributes relate to Colombian engineering students' results on the Saber 11 (end of high school) and Saber Pro (end of university) examinations.

Author: Qingan Xu · November 2026
